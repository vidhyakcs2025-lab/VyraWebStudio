OUR CRAFT

Rooted in intention, poured with passion.

LUMA was born out of a simple ritual: the pause between the morning rush. We believe that true coffee craft requires patience — from our direct farmer partnerships in Karnataka and Ethiopia to our custom profile roasts.

Every pastry is baked before sunrise. Every cup is dialed in with precise water mineral chemistry. LUMA is your quiet neighborhood sanctuary for thoughtful conversations, quiet work sessions, and nourishing meals.

✓ 100% Arabica micro-lots scored 86+ Specialty Coffee Association
✓ Oat, almond, and dairy options steamed to silky microfoam
✓ Dog-friendly sunlit veranda & high-speed workstation tables
